ABOUT THE HOUSE

Five real auction lots. One purse. How close can you bid?

How to play

🔨 Bid ≥ the real price → you win it (and pay your bid)
💨 Bid under → someone else gets it, your money's safe
🎯 Closer = more points, up to 100 per lot
💸 Overpay early and you're broke for the rest

Is it real?

Every lot really sold at auction: dinosaurs, diamonds, Ferraris, a $4 million cow. Prices include the buyer's premium. New catalogue every midnight.

Paddle is an independent game and isn't affiliated with any auction house. Our engravings are illustrations, not photos.
