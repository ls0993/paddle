PRIVACY

Last updated: 17 November 2026

What we keep

Paddle has no accounts and asks for no personal information. Your results, streak and stats are saved only in your own browser's local storage, on your device. We never receive them, and clearing your browser data deletes them.

When you finish the daily sale, your score for that day (a number from 0 to 500) is sent to our server, so we can tell every player how they did compared with everyone else. Nothing that identifies you or your device is stored with it.

Third parties

The game's fonts are served by Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to download them. Google's privacy policy covers that connection.
